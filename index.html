Welcome to my creative writing multimedia project!

This is part assignment and part passion project that I've been wanting to get started on for a long time. Kimmy's World is a website-narrative/hypertext fiction heavily inspired by the work of Kane Parsons and the style of other ARGs/multimedia narratives such as 17776, Welcome Home, and Mystery Flesh Pit National Park (all referenced below).

Kimmy's World is a webpage frozen in the late 90s, created and run by a 16 year old girl named Kimberly Evans, who mysteriously vanished in the year 1998. I wanted to capture the essence of late 90s to early 2000s web design and internet culture

While I plan to expand on the concept, there is currently only one linear narrative that you can follow by clicking through the site however you choose. The links will either open in the same tab or a new one by design, to let you "surf" easier. Any broken links are done purposely and will simply not redirect you at all. Any visual glitches are part of the narrative. You can view the site's underlying code by right clicking the page and selecting 'Page Source'.
